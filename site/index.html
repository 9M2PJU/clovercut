Why creators switch to Clovercut

Native Rust & WGSL Speed

Zero Electron overhead. Built entirely in Rust with native Slint UI and WGSL GPU compute shaders for instantaneous frame seeking and smooth playback.

Single-Key Cut (Ctrl+B)

CapCut-faithful single track cutting. Split clips right beneath your playhead instantly without clicking tools or losing your creative momentum.

On-Device Whisper AI Captions

Accurate, automated transcription in 90+ languages right on your machine. No monthly subscriptions, no limits, and your recordings never leave your drive.

1:1 Square Previews & Stills

Beautiful CapCut-style square shelf cards with Sunset and City preview stills, making effects, color filters, and transitions intuitive to browse.

Adjustment Layers & Masks

Non-destructive color grading and effects stacked above video tracks with strict timeline constraints to keep your edit tree organized and reliable.

Desktop & Mobile Parity

One pure Rust core compiled across Windows, Linux AppImage (x86_64 and ARM64), and Android smartphones/tablets.
