# Clovercut 0.1.2

Clovercut 0.1.2 introduces CapCut-style timeline track zones, faithful workspace layout presets, professional NLE keyboard shortcut profiles, and upstream engine reliability enhancements.

## Highlights & Major Features

### CapCut-Style Timeline Track Zones & Fixed Main Track
- **Fixed Main Video Track:** New projects start with exactly 1 primary video track (`is_main: true`) that cannot be deleted or pruned.
- **Dedicated Audio Zone (Below Main Track):** Audio clips and tracks are strictly constrained below the main video track, allowing clean layering and stacking of audio below visual content.
- **Dedicated Visual Zone (Above Main Track):** Video overlays, text titles, captions, stickers, and filters are strictly placed on or above the main video track.
- **Smooth Track Insertion:** Eliminated track shifting jitter during drag operations; drag-and-drop now renders a clean glowing insertion indicator with an inline `+ Nova Pista` badge at the exact drop target line (`insert_y`), allowing placement on top, bottom, or between existing lanes.

### Faithful CapCut Workspace Layout Presets
- **Quick Layout Switcher:** Dedicated dropdown menu in the top-right title bar with checkmarks and instant layout resetting.
- **Criação de Áudio (Audio Editing):** 100% full-height center timeline with waveforms and VU meters, library and player stacked on the left, and inspector on the right.
- **Vídeo Vertical (Direita) (Vertical Video - Right):** Full-height player on the right for 9:16 vertical video workflows (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), with media & inspector stacked on top-left and timeline on bottom-left.
- **Vídeo Vertical (Esquerda) (Vertical Video - Left):** Full-height player on the left, media & inspector stacked on top-right, and timeline on bottom-right.
- **Layout Padrão (Default Layout):** Balanced upper workspace (Media, Player, Inspector) with full-width bottom timeline.
- **Biblioteca Prioritária (Media Priority):** Wide media shelf (~48% width) for browsing large asset catalogues.
- **Inspetor Prioritário (Inspector Priority):** Expanded inspector controls (~48% width) for fine-grained color and text editing.

### Professional NLE Shortcut Profiles
- **Header Keyboard Shortcut Button:** Dedicated keyboard icon in the top-right header for one-click access to shortcuts configuration.
- **Pre-configured Industry Profiles:**
  - **Clovercut / CapCut (Padrão):** Native CapCut hotkeys (`Ctrl+B` split, `Q`/`W` ripple trim head/tail, `B` split tool, `Delete`/`Shift+Delete`).
  - **Adobe Premiere Pro:** `Ctrl+K` Add Edit, `C` Razor tool, `S` Snap toggle, `Ctrl+M` Export, `Q`/`W` Ripple trim.
  - **Final Cut Pro:** `Ctrl+B`/`B` Blade, `[`/`]` Trim start/end, `N` Snap toggle, `V` Disable/Mute clip.
  - **DaVinci Resolve:** `Ctrl+\` Split clip, `B` Blade tool, `N` Snap toggle, `D` Disable clip, `Shift+Backspace` Ripple delete.
  - **Personalizado (Custom):** Automatically selected whenever keybindings are customized.

### Upstream Concat Reliability & Core Improvements
- **Focused Sheet & Text Input Isolation:** Typing in input fields or modal sheets (Settings, Export) no longer triggers timeline editing shortcuts.
- **Atomic Preferences Persistence:** Thread-safe private preferences writing with graceful salvage recovery for corrupt or partial configs.
- **Android Display & Safe Mode:** OpenGL ES fallback for Android devices with unstable Vulkan drivers.
- **Media Encoding & Protocol Upgrades:** Updated FFmpeg and ONNX runtime integration.
